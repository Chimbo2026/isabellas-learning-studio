Saturday filming plan · Easy pitch guide

A grown-up host and Isabella as hands-on co-host · about 15–20 minutes
🎒
Gather a piano or your voices, plus a quiet place to listen.

Keep the clips short and let Isabella help choose the examples. No retakes needed.

👋
Welcome · What is pitch?
Ask: “Can a sound feel high or low?”
🎹
Demonstrate high and low
Play two notes on the piano or use your voice. Show a simple visual as you explain.
🙌
Play the listening game
Take turns making a sound; Isabella guesses high or low, then makes one for you.
💬
Recap together
Ask what she noticed and celebrate her explanation.

Today’s little goal

Connect a new word to something Isabella enjoys, then try it in a sentence.

  1. Draw an idea for a video, picture, or game Isabella would like to make.
  2. Introduce vision: an idea or picture in your mind of what you hope to create.
  3. Explain exchange: give one thing and receive another. Act it out by swapping picture cards.
  4. Invite Isabella to say: “My vision is…” or “Let’s exchange ideas.” Check together whether each word is new, practising, or known.
