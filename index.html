Our four-scene video plan

A grown-up host and Isabella as hands-on co-host
👋
1 · Host introduces the idea
Speak to the camera: “Today we’re exploring high and low pitch.”
🎹
2 · Show it clearly
Use your voice, piano keys, simple drawings, or on-screen labels to explain.
🙌
3 · Try it together
Pause for Isabella to listen, guess, clap a rhythm, or play an example.
💬
4 · Recap and say goodbye
Ask Isabella what she noticed, repeat the main idea, and wave goodbye.
